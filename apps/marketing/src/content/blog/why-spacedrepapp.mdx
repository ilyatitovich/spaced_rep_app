Anki changed how millions of people study. It is also famously hard to start.

## The gap

Power users love decks, note types, add-ons, and fine-tuned intervals. Many learners open Anki once, bounce off the interface, and conclude spaced repetition “isn’t for them.”

That is a product problem, not a science problem.

## What SpacedRepApp optimizes for

- **Everyday study** — short sessions, clear levels, come back tomorrow.
- **Local-first** — your device holds the truth; sync is optional.
- **Less ceremony** — rich cards without a weekend of configuration.
- **Import when you need it** — bring Anki decks if you already have them.

## Inspired by clear teaching

The review schedule is inspired by approaches like Nicky Case’s explainer on remembering forever-ish: make the mechanism legible, keep the habit humane.

## Who it is for

If you want to remember languages, exams, lectures, or craft knowledge — and you want a calm daily loop more than a research-grade SRS lab — SpacedRepApp is built for you.
