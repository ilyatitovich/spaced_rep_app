AI can draft, summarize, and search. That does not mean **you** remember anything.

## Answers are cheap; ownership is not

When every question has an instant answer, it is easy to outsource thinking. You feel informed. A week later the detail is gone — and you cannot rebuild it without opening the tool again.

The people who compound skill are the ones who decide what is worth keeping in their own head, then practice retrieving it.

## Manage attention like a scarce resource

Notifications, feeds, and tab sprawl fragment encoding. If you never hold an idea long enough to encode it, no spaced-repetition schedule can save you.

Protect short blocks of undivided attention for learning. Then use a review system so that investment does not evaporate.

## What AI is good for here

- Turning dense material into candidate flashcards you still edit.
- Explaining a concept once so you can write a cleaner prompt.
- Not replacing the retrieval reps that make knowledge yours.

## The durable skill

Learn fast enough to keep up — and **remember** deliberately. Attention plus spacing is how knowledge survives the feed.
